This special edition follows the Angry Birds films from the first island adventure through the sequel and toward a third theatrical story, celebrating the characters, friendships, and fandom along the way.

01

THE BEGINNING · 2016

The Angry Birds Movie

A colorful island, an unlikely team

The first film brought the birds and pigs into a lively animated setting, mixing comedy, conflict, friendship, and adventure.

Red stands apart from the rest of the island, and his point of view becomes central as events begin to unfold.

When Chuck and Bomb join him, the three friends face the arrival of the pigs and a growing change in how the two groups relate.

The movie introduced game-familiar characters to a feature-film audience and established a cinematic world of its own.

THE FRIENDSHIP

Red, Chuck & Bomb

Three very different personalities form a team, with their friendship anchoring much of the story.

THE ARRIVAL

The pigs

The visitors' arrival unsettles island life and sets the central conflict in motion.

02

THE STORY GROWS · 2019

The Angry Birds Movie 2

A challenge that changes the rules

The sequel takes the world of the first film further and gives its cast a larger problem to solve.

Birds and pigs find themselves in an unexpected position where cooperation becomes necessary.

Building on the original's characters and rivalries, the second movie widens the setting and continues the cinematic story.

03

THE NEXT CHAPTER · 2026

The Angry Birds Movie 3

FEATURE STORY

A new era for the flock

The third movie carries the series into another chapter, following two films that introduced its characters, friendships, rivalries, and adventures to audiences.

For longtime viewers, another cinema outing means a chance to reconnect with familiar faces while finding out what new situations await them.

The return also gives fans another moment to celebrate the animated universe on the big screen and its place among today's movie stories.

For PewJay Official, the film connects with a wider creative path shaped by digital art, games, animation, livestreams, fandom, and movie-inspired ideas.
